# 🏋️ Spiritual Gym: 7 Exercises Tracker

> *Reps + Consistency = Stronger Relationship with Allah*

A beautiful, interactive web app to track your 7 daily spiritual exercises across the week, built to help Muslims build consistency in worship through a familiar "gym workout" framework.

https://nounnzz.github.io/spiritual-gym/

---

## 🕌 The 7 Spiritual Exercises

| # | Exercise | Goal | When |
|---|----------|------|------|
| 1 | **The 4 T's** *(Tasbiḥ, Taḥmīd, Takbīr, Tahlīl)* | After each Salah | 5x daily |
| 2 | **Istighfar** | 1,000x a day | Anytime |
| 3 | **Salawat** | 100x a day | Anytime |
| 4 | **Dua** | Sujood of Salah | Each prayer |
| 5 | **Quran** | 1 page a day | Daily |
| 6 | **Tahajjud** | 2 units | Before Fajr |
| 7 | **Athkar** | Morning & Evening | After Fajr / After Asr |

---

## ✨ Features

- 📅 **Weekly Tracker**: Check off each exercise for every day of the week (Mon–Sun)
- 📊 **Progress Bar**: Visual weekly completion progress updated in real time
- 💡 **Virtues Panel**: Each exercise card lists its spiritual benefits and virtues
- 📤 **Share Your Progress**: Share your weekly workout results with others
- 🔄 **Start New Week**: Reset and begin a fresh week of tracking
- 📱 **Responsive Design**: Works seamlessly on desktop and mobile

## 🧠 How It Works

1. Each of the 7 exercise cards displays a **Mon–Sun checkbox grid**
2. Checking a day marks that exercise as completed for that day
3. The **progress bar** at the top updates dynamically based on total checkboxes completed
4. Your progress is saved in **localStorage** so it persists between sessions
5. Clicking **Start New Week** clears all checkboxes to begin a fresh week
6. **Share Your Progress** lets you export or share your weekly stats
